Who We Are

Our Values

YouTalk is a well-structured platform that brings together young people from diverse communities, equipping them with the skills to excel academically, grow professionally, and maintain positive mental well-being to thrive in the global space.

Professionalism

We uphold the highest standards of professional conduct, ethics, and expertise in all our interactions.

Inclusivity

Creating an environment where every young person feels welcome, respected, and valued regardless of background.

Diversity

Celebrating and embracing differences in culture, perspective, and experience to enrich our community.

Our Services

Core Sessions Designed for You

Three specialised sessions addressing different aspects of personal and professional growth

Teen Talk

Teen Talk

Young people participate in interactive group sessions led by an educational psychologist or counsellor, where they are empowered on a wide range of topics

  • Adolescent Rights
  • Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR)
  • Significance of Education
  • Academic Challenges
  • Journaling
  • Peer Pressure
  • Family Conflicts
  • Health & Wellness
  • Relationships
  • Personal Development
Guidance & Counselling

Guidance & Counselling (G&C)

A supportive process empowering young people to develop self-awareness, positive self-image, and navigate life's challenges.

  • Emotional & mental well-being
  • Self-esteem building
  • Personal development
  • Values & morality
Career Development

Career Development (CD)

A well-structured programme that guides young people in making informed academic and career decisions, preparing them for transitions into senior high school, tertiary education, or vocational skills training.

  • Program selection aligned with career goals
  • Understanding job prospects
  • Practical skills for employment
  • Business creation opportunities
